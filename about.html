VÉLODROME IS A CREATIVE DIRECTION OFFICE THAT GIVES IDEAS A WORLD OF THEIR OWN. WE WORK TO FIND WHAT MAKES EACH PROJECT DISTINCT, AND THEN BUILD A LANGUAGE AROUND IT. FROM LIVE EXPERIENCES AND MOVING IMAGE TO VISUAL IDENTITY, EVERY PART BELONGS TO THE SAME WORLD.

WHAT WE DO

CREATIVE DIRECTION

EVERY PROJECT BEGINS WITH A POINT OF VIEW. WE FIND THE IDEA AT ITS CORE AND SHAPE A WORLD AROUND IT: ITS STORY, VISUAL LANGUAGE AND THE FEELING IT LEAVES BEHIND. ONE VISION THAT GIVES EVERY EXPRESSION OF THE PROJECT A REASON TO BELONG.

LIVE

A LIVE EXPERIENCE SHOULD STAY WITH YOU AFTER YOU LEAVE. WE TURN IDEAS INTO SPACES, MOMENTS AND MOVEMENT, FROM ARTIST SHOWS TO EVENTS AND BRAND EXPERIENCES. EVERY ELEMENT PLAYS A PART IN THE SAME STORY, AND THE AUDIENCE BECOMES PART OF IT TOO.

AUDIOVISUAL

MOVING IMAGE LETS A WORLD REVEAL ITSELF OVER TIME. WE DEVELOP CONCEPTS FOR MUSIC VIDEOS, VISUALIZERS AND OTHER AUDIOVISUAL PIECES, GIVING EACH ONE ITS OWN RHYTHM, ATMOSPHERE AND POINT OF VIEW WHILE KEEPING IT CONNECTED TO THE WIDER PROJECT.

VISUAL IDENTITY

AN IDENTITY IS MORE THAN A RECOGNIZABLE LOOK. IT’S A LANGUAGE A PROJECT CAN SPEAK IN MANY PLACES AND STILL SOUND LIKE ITSELF. WE DESIGN VISUAL SYSTEMS FOR ARTISTS, BRANDS AND CULTURAL PROJECTS THAT HOLD TOGETHER ACROSS ARTWORKS, CAMPAIGNS, MERCH AND BEYOND.
